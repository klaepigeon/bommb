# Flat 47: beat sheet

Every spoken line is in `data/dialogue/*.json`, verbatim from the script. Stage directions are
`event` steps the levels act on. Nothing else is said.

## Level 1: Flat 47

| # | Beat | How it plays | Lines |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Arrival | Black, no text. Knocking fades in on the landing outside 47. | `knock` |
| 2 | Inside | Kurt opens the door himself. Entering the hall. | `see_flat` "Christ..." |
| 3 | Marta | Seen from the living room, in her chair in the corner under her lamp. | `see_marta` |
| 4 | Look closer | Use her: he goes to her and kneels. She stirs, talks, turns to him. | `investigate_marta` |
| 5 | The grab | She comes up out of the chair at him; he gasps; she throws him across the room into the wall. Black. | (event `grab`) |
| 6 | Blackout | Heartbeat. The front door slams. She's gone when he wakes. | |
| 7 | Waking | On his back. He lifts his left hand to his head: it's a sock. It turns round and talks. | `wake` |
| 8 | The door | Shut, and it gives a little and stops: something outside holds it. | `door_locked` |
| 9 | The wardrobe | Blue goo under it; one of Socko's eyes keeps going to it. Push it: the hole. | `wardrobe_hole` (cross-eyed; he points to the kitchen) |
| 10 | The pistol | Kitchen drawer. Four rounds. | |
| 11 | The balcony | Unlocked now. Beyond the railing, a sticky note that says "outside". | `balcony` |
| 12 | Into the hole | He crawls in. Blue light, then black, then the loading card. | |

## Level 2: Flat 47, again

| # | Beat | How it plays | Lines |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Out of the wall | He slides out of a fleshy hole onto the floor of a room that is nearly her bedroom. | |
| 2 | The maze | Her rooms, her armchair, her wardrobes and her front doors, repeated and wrongly joined, gone to meat. | |
| 3 | Marta | The first time they see each other she stops and clicks at him. Then she hunts. | `marta_maze` |
| 4 | Out | The real front door. Across the landing, 48 is open. Card: Flat 48. | |

Between beats, when it's quiet: `random_a`, `random_b`, `random_c` (C depends on memories invested).

## What's deliberately unexplained

The boxes. Why Marta calls him "my little bird", and how she knows him. Who held the door.
Where Socko came from (he has a theory). What the goo is. What's past the sticky note.
