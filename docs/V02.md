Shared frameworks: `ArenaGame` (movement, push, carry, stun, cold spots) and `ChoiceGame`
(secret choices, claims, peeks, reveal). Every game lists its own buttons
(`actions_for`), so the HUD and the AI need no per-game code.

**Not done / lighter than the spec** (next):
- Leaving the table is hotspot-to-hotspot (your person walks there; you act; two actions;
  back) rather than free first-person walking.
- Ghost "communication with other ghosts" is a single quick line.
- Minigame visuals are functional (shared arena / choice views), not bespoke per game.
- Balance: numbers are first passes (all in JSON).
- Everything here is tested on one machine; not yet on phones.
