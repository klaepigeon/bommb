# HYSTERIA — Flat 47 Story Bible and Opening Beat Sheet

Deliverable 1. Status: **draft for approval.** Everything marked **PROPOSED** is a decision the
master prompt leaves open; it is written down here so it can be approved or changed before
anything is built on it. Nothing in the established opening (prompt §3.1) has been altered.

---

## 1. Setting

- **Kerzhansk, Russia, October 1996.** A mid-sized industrial city (fictional). Building 14,
  Ulitsa Gagarina: a 1950s brick block, nine storeys, on a courtyard.
- The district around Plant No. 3 was **evacuated on 15 October**: buses from the school yard at
  06:00, one bag each, tap water declared unsafe, "do not go down to the basements". The official
  line is a precautionary water-contamination measure. (Already in the game's environmental
  text; dates fit 1996. The notice's issuer becomes the city administration rather than a Soviet
  "District Executive Committee" — **PROPOSED** wording fix for 1996.)
- Most residents left. Some didn't. Something has been growing in the building since.

## 2. Characters

### The protagonist: Kurt Federov
- Owner's canon (full detail in `docs/KURT.md`): **Kurt Federov**, German-Russian, 177.8 cm, an
  older retired man in 1996. Circus performer, then assassin, then a superhero in the
  **Champion's League**, which was absorbed into the **United Front**; since then, freelance
  investigative work. Dry humour, patience, observation, compassion, restraint, and the capacity
  for extremely efficient violence.
- **Why he is at Flat 47:** a wellness check. (The newer documents say the woman is *Sonya, an old
  villain from his past*; this bible follows the owner's confirmation that she is Marta. See
  `docs/KURT.md` section 2: unresolved.)
- **After the attack:** no memory of who he is, why he's here, or his past. His body remembers:
  balance, economy of movement, how to hold a weapon, how to stay low and quiet, how to weigh an
  object for throwing. He is surprised by his own competence and never explains it.
- Age shows as generic stiffness (neck, shoulders, hands; the hand flexing). No specific
  injuries: the "stiff left shoulder, bad right knee" of the first draft are withdrawn.

### Marta Sokolova
- **Owner's canon (this session; supersedes the husband and the "forty-one"):** a **psychic**,
  once the **lover of Rasputin**. Decades later she **fought Trickshot** (Kurt) and became **his
  lover**. **Kurt is the father of her daughter Sonya, and abandoned Sonya as a child.** There is
  no husband in Flat 47: the flat is Marta's and Sonya's. All of this is told **only through the
  environment** (the labyrinth behind the bedroom wardrobe): nobody says it.
- **Open questions (not invented here):** her age (Rasputin died in December 1916; she looks the
  same in his photograph as at her table); Sonya's age now and when Kurt left; whether "the woman
  in 47" of the newer documents (Sonya, "an old villain") is reconciled by this. The flat's
  child's room, the calendar ("the 22nd") and the fridge note were written for a seven-year-old
  Sonya and are left as they are until her age is decided.
- **Her condition:** to every sense, a corpse three weeks gone, still in her place at the dinner
  table. The flesh has gone soft and is sliding off her: into her lap, off the chair in strings,
  into a black puddle spreading under the table. Grey-green and purple-black, marbled, mould in
  pale fur. Coral (flesh-red at the root, pale at the tips) has grown out of her back, shoulders,
  arms and face and down through the chair into the floor; her forearms are fused to the cloth.
  Her lips are gone and her cheeks have split back toward her ears, so she grins, very wide, at
  her plate. Milky eyes that still catch the light.
- **She is not dead.** She doesn't breathe, doesn't twitch; only the flies move. The first time
  she moves must be the last thing the player expects.

### Socko
- A dirty striped sock with big googly eyes and a red felt tongue, worn on the protagonist's
  left hand. Appears **only** after the protagonist wakes.
- **What Socko claims:** that he's always been there ("Course I know you, bud. I'm on your
  hand."); that he's here to help get them both out.
- **Who Socko is (owner's canon, withheld in Flat 47):** Socko is **Psygmund**. Nothing in
  Flat 47 names, hints at, or explains Psygmund; his nature beyond that is the owner's to define.
- **What Socko actually knows (withheld in Flat 47):** the layout of the flat, where useful
  things are, what the infection does, and who the protagonist used to be. He uses that
  knowledge selectively.
- **Agenda:** he wants the protagonist violent, obedient and incurious. Every shortcut he offers
  is the loud, destructive, cruel one. He is never openly evil in Flat 47 — friendly, funny,
  useful, a little impatient when the player looks at things too long.

### ~~Ilya Sokolov~~ (removed by the owner)
- There is no husband. The two pieces of flat text that named him are rewritten in the game
  (`flat47.gd` TEXT_PATCH): the wedding photograph now has the photograph cut out of the frame;
  the letters are Marta's own, to a post-box with no name, all returned. Who lived in the box
  room (the den, the tally marks, the bolt) is **withheld**; the bolt is still the one "someone
  used".

### Sonya — the heart of HYSTERIA
- Marta's and Kurt's daughter. **Finding Sonya is the real heart of the game.** Her nursery is in
  the labyrinth, with a door marked СОНЯ, scratched low down at a child's height, that won't
  open. Where she is, and what she is now, is withheld.

### Kurt is Trickshot
- Kurt's hero name in the Champion's League was **Trickshot** (owner's canon): the bow and trick
  arrows. The old **PROPOSED** codename "Strelok" is withdrawn; Socko's slip when Marta dies is
  now *"Trickshot's still got it!"*.

## 2b. The labyrinth behind the wardrobe (owner's canon; layout and props PROPOSED)

Shouldering the **bedroom wardrobe** aside along the kitchen wall uncovers a ragged hole in the
plaster. The kitchen is on the other side of that wall; the hole goes somewhere else. Through it
is an **impossible labyrinth: Kerzhansk as it was under the Romanovs** (about 1910): cobbled
streets, ochre façades with carved window frames, gas lamps, pre-reform signs, a church dome that
stays the same distance away however far you walk, streets that come back to where they began.
Here Kurt meets Marta again (hunting him by sound, floating, still seated in a chair that isn't
there) and finds **the gun**. Everything about who she was is told by places and things:

| Place | What it shows |
|---|---|
| Séance salon (ФОТОГРАФІЯ above it) | chairs and teacups circling in the air; a studio photograph of Rasputin with his hand on her head; his note ("You saw the river. You told me first. — G."); the Petrograd paper of December 1916 |
| The frozen fight (a square) | a fight stopped mid-moment: paving stones and chairs hanging in the air, trick arrows frozen in flight toward one point, a black domino mask on the cobbles, a Champion's League poster: TRICKSHOT. The revolver lies in the middle of it |
| A hotel room (ГОСТИНИЦА) | a bed, a quiver over the chair, two glasses (one with lipstick), a photo-booth strip of the two of them |
| The nursery | a cradle rocking by itself, a drawing of MAMA and a small man with a bow walking off the edge of the paper, letters to "Papa" never posted, a birth register with the father written in and scratched out; the door СОНЯ |

**The gun (PROPOSED):** a **Nagant M1895 revolver**, seven rounds, loaded a round at a time (it
belongs to the labyrinth's era; Kurt's hands know it). No firearm canon existed to use instead.

## 2c. Socko in the labyrinth (owner's direction; lines new, PROPOSED wording)

Socko wants Marta dead and the tutorial rushed: no reading, no looking, take the gun, take the
nuggets, buy the headshot. He guides Kurt straight to the revolver and pushes the skill tree's
**Headshot** (instant kill). He's dismissive of every clue and goes quiet in the nursery.

**Socko's labyrinth lines (built; new, PROPOSED wording, owner to approve):**
- The gap: *"Huh. Would you look at that. A shortcut."* · Arriving: *"Don't stop. Don't look.
  Something of yours is down here. This way."* · Steering (repeats, with a point down the way):
  *"This way, bud."* / *"Keep going. Don't read the signs."* / *"You don't need to see that. Gun
  first."* / *"Left, right, whatever. Come on."*
- Clues, waved away: photograph *"Old photo. Dead people. Come on."* · note *"Don't read other
  people's mail, bud."* · newspaper *"Old news. Literally."* · poster *"Huh. Some fella. Gun's that
  way."* · mask *"Nope. Not your colour. Put it down."* · quiver *"Arrows? We're past arrows. We've
  got a gun."* · photo strip *"…Anyway."* · glasses *"Somebody had a nice night. Come on."* ·
  register *"Paperwork. Boring."* · letters *"Nobody's reading those, bud."* · Sonya's door
  *"Leave it."* · the nursery: dead still and silent, then *"Nothing in here. Let's go."* (a lie).
- The revolver: the bible's *"Ooh. Now we're talking. You know what that's for."* and, reloading,
  *"Look at you go. Did you see your hands? I didn't teach you that."*
- Marta: *"There she is. Don't think. Aim for the head."* · hits *"Again! Again!"* / *"Oh, that's
  it. That's it!"* · after she takes hold of him *"Up. Up. She's had her go. Now we have ours."* ·
  her death *"Trickshot's still got it!"* … *"What? It's a… word. Come on."*
- Nuggets and the tree: *"Ooh, shiny. Grab those. Grab all of those."* · *"You've got enough.
  Headshot. Trust me."* · bought Headshot *"There we go. Now we're friends."* · bought a Memory
  skill *"…Really? That? Okay. Your nuggets."*

Marta in the labyrinth (built): she comes into the town a few seconds after the revolver is
taken, floating, seated in nothing, with a cold light about her; she hunts by sound (footsteps by
how he moves, impacts, the scrape of the wardrobe, gunshots across the whole town) and by sight
(further with his torch on, less when he's crouched); if she reaches him she takes hold of him
("Have you seen them?") and he comes to by the hole; shot, she staggers (body 1, head 3 of 8);
with Headshot a head shot kills her, and that's permanent. Close by she murmurs: *"…the
boxes…"*, *"Have you seen…"*, *"Sonya, shoes."* (the bible's fragments).

**Gutterspace nuggets:** floating pink-and-black shards that look like missing textures,
scattered through the flat and the labyrinth. They're spent in the skill tree, where **anything
can be bought in any order** (Memory or Survival). Survival skills make Marta more aggressive;
Memory skills calm her and reveal more of the story.

**What the Memory skills give back (built; wording new, PROPOSED):**
- *Her Handwriting* — the written clues say more. Note: the folds are hers, small enough for a
  glove; she had it on her the night you met her. Newspaper: in the margin, in her pencil,
  «Told him.» Photo strip, on the back: «T. and me. The last good night.» Drawing, on the back:
  «She asked me where he went. I said far.» Sonya's letters: the P of PAPA is made the way you
  make yours; you showed her how. Register: under the crossings-out, a K and an F — she wrote
  you in, and then she couldn't leave you there.
- *How She Moves* — near Marta, a low pressure in the ears, louder as she nears, from where she
  is; the first time: *"You know where she is. Not how. Behind you, the way you know where your
  own hand is."*
- *These Streets* — a chalk circle with a cross through it on the cobbles where the main street
  comes round; the first time through the seam: *"The same chalk circle on the same cobbles.
  You've come round. The street goes on for ever, and it's thirteen houses long."* Socko:
  *"Streets are like that. Keep walking."*
- *Counting* — every minute or so, from the direction of the nursery (never inside it), a
  girl's voice counting in Russian, hide-and-seek: *«…семь, восемь, девять, десять. Я иду
  искать.»* It stops when you listen for it. Socko goes dead still.

**Saving (built):** one quiet slot. It saves every 30 s once Socko is on the hand, on pause, and
when the app is put away; launching continues. "Restart chapter", `-- fresh`, and
finishing the chapter wipe it.

## 3. The boxes (the question is load-bearing)

**Owner's direction:** the boxes are **not physical objects**, and they come from **somewhere else
in the story** (to be defined by the owner). Nothing in Flat 47 may suggest they are crates,
parcels, luggage or anything you could find in a room.

Rules for Flat 47:
- Marta asks the question as if it were the most important thing in the world, and as if any
  reasonable person would know what she means.
- No object, document or line in Flat 47 explains or illustrates the boxes.
- The protagonist doesn't understand the question. Neither does the player. Socko changes the
  subject (bible §6) — the one thing he won't joke about.
- Integration point for later: a narrative flag `heard_boxes_question` and the exact wording of
  every line that mentions boxes are recorded so the larger story can pay them off.

## 5. Opening beat sheet

Timings are targets for pacing, not hard limits.

1. **Arrival (cutscene, ~40 s).** Courtyard at dusk, lobby, stairs, landing. The door of 47
   stands ajar. He knocks: "Mrs Sokolova? My name is Federov. I'm here to look in on you." [the job's client
   and wording: **PROPOSED**, pending the Marta/Sonya question] He goes in. The door swings shut behind him. *(No chain: he is not trapped yet.)*
2. **Initial exploration (player control, 2–4 min).** Hallway, kitchen, bedroom open; the
   living-room door is half shut. He reads the flat: the calendar, the fridge note, the water
   jars, the suitcase. A smell, and a slow wet sound of breathing from the living room.
3. **Discovering the apartment's condition.** Mould climbing the hall wall toward the living room;
   coral nubs in the skirting; the dinner table seen through the frosted glass of the door.
4. **The woman.** He opens the living-room door. Marta at the table, fused to her chair, her back
   to him, coral standing out of it against the window light; from behind, plainly dead. The player can walk round her, look at her, look at the
   table. **No timer.** Nothing happens until the player leans in to look at her face, or touches
   her (or lingers in the room ~45 s).
5. **The question.** Her head *snaps* up to him, a crack of bone and coral, the jaw dropping open
   on the grin; he's thrown back a step. Then she simply watches him, her head following him round
   the room a beat behind. Quiet, polite, tired: *"Have you seen the boxes?"* A pause the player can fill by
   walking away or staying. If he stays, she asks again, more carefully, as if he might not
   have heard: *"The boxes. Have you seen them?"* If he answers by looking at her (interact):
   *"You haven't. No. Nobody has."*
6. **The attack.** Once she's asked, coming within reach is enough: without warning — no sting
   before it — her arm tears up off the table, strings of her stretching and snapping, coral
   bursting out of the cloth, and closes on his wrist. The chair shrieks across the floor as she pulls him down to her.
   Camera driven by his body: yanked forward and down, his free hand braced on the table, plates
   going over. Her face close: *"Have you seen them?"* Darkness from the edges in.
7. **Blackout (~5 s of near silence).** A heartbeat, the building's hum, a child's
   counting-under-breath that may be the pipes.
8. **Waking.** On the living-room floor beside the table, looking at the ceiling. Blurred vision
   clearing. He sits up badly (shoulder). The chair is empty: torn upholstery, coral stumps, mould
   in the shape of where she sat. **She is gone.** He doesn't know where he is, or who.
9. **The hand.** His left hand rises into view on its own, slowly. There is a sock on it. Googly
   eyes. It looks at him.
10. **Socko's introduction (~60 s, not rushed).** Socko speaks. Physical comedy and unease in
    equal measure. He gives a reason to move and nothing else. (Dialogue §6.)
11. **Tutorial and the threat.** The front door now **is** chained and padlocked from the inside
    — and he has no memory of doing it. Marta is loose in the flat: infected, mobile, hunting by
    sound. The player learns movement, interaction, inspection, carrying and throwing, the
    pistol, noise and stealth while finding a way out. Socko keeps nudging toward the gun.
12. **Escape.** Any mix of these (systemic, not scripted routes):
    - **Loud:** shoot the padlock (two rounds; brings her running), or take the axe from the box
      room to the chain (three blows, very loud). Socko's preference: kill her first.
    - **Quiet:** the padlock key is at the bottom of the black bath (drain it, ~30 s of gurgling
      noise you have to manage). Or lure her with a thrown object / the radio / the TV into the box
      room and **slide the bolt**. *(The old line tied the bolt to Ilya; he is gone from canon.)*
    - **Mixed:** wound her and bolt her in; distract her and shoot the lock while she's away.
    Leaving: the chain falls, the landing, the stairwell. Socko: *"See? Easy. Next time we just
    do it my way."* — or, if she was killed, he is delighted and the player should feel it.

    *Built (Godot, `scripts/world/flat_escape.gd`):* the chain and padlock, the axe (three blows,
    each heard as a 0.85 noise), the padlock (two rounds), the bath (pull the plug, ~30 s,
    gurgles every 2.5–4 s heard as 0.42; the keys and the right shoe in the silt; the keys unlock
    the padlock quietly). Through the door: the white, Socko's line, the end card. The bolt-trap
    route waits on Marta being loose in the flat.

**End of chapter.** Fade on the landing. The peephole of 48 opposite is dark, then not.

## 6. Dialogue (first draft)

Delivery notes in brackets. Socko's lines are written to be performed with the hand, not read.

### Marta
- **Before the question (breathing only).**
- *"Have you seen the boxes?"* [soft, ordinary, the way you'd ask a neighbour about the post]
- *"The boxes. Have you seen them?"* [patient, careful, as to someone hard of hearing]
- *"You haven't. No. Nobody has."* [a little disappointed; not sinister]
- **Attack:** *"Have you seen them?"* [very close; urgent; human — the same question, begged]
- **Infected, hunting (fragments, overheard):** "Sonya, shoes." / "Have you seen…" / counting
  under her breath. [the same voice, broken up; never shouting]

### Socko — introduction (beat 10)
1. [the hand rises; long silence; the mouth opens a little, closes] *"…Hi."*
2. [leans in, looks him over] *"Oof. Okay. You took a knock. Don't sit up fast."*
3. [he sits up anyway; Socko winces] *"Or do. Sure. You do you."*
4. [player looks at him] *"It's me. Socko. Your hand? We're close."* [beat] *"Physically."*
5. [player looks round the room] *"She's gone. Don't look at the chair, bud. Look at me."*
6. [turns toward the hall] *"Door's that way. Let's get you out before she comes back for
   seconds."*

### Socko — tutorial (contextual, first time only; keyboard or pad wording)
- Movement: *"Walk it off. Slow. Your knee's making noises I don't like."*
- Crouch: *"Get low. Lower. There you go — you've done this before, huh?"*
- Inspect: *"Turn it over. Yeah. Fascinating. Can we go?"* [impatient seed]
- Pick up / throw: *"Heavy end first. Throw it like you mean it. Or — you know where it'd really
  land? On her."* [violent seed]
- Noise: *"Hear that? She hears it too. Everything you drop, she hears."*
- The pistol (found in Ilya's jacket lining **PROPOSED**): *"Ooh. Now we're talking. You know
  what that's for."* [delighted] — reloading: *"Look at you go. Did you see your hands? I didn't
  teach you that."* [the first hint he knows something]
- Bath / key: *"Or. Or. We shoot the lock. Takes two seconds. The bath takes forever."*
- Bolt option: *"You're gonna shut her in a cupboard? Like him?"* [irritated; slips: "like him"]
- If the player spares her: *"…Fine. Fine. Your way. Slower, but fine."* [passive-aggressive]
- If the player kills her: *"There we are. Much simpler."* [delighted; the player should feel it]

## 7. Tone rules

- Marta is frightening because of what has happened to her and how ordinary she sounds.
- Socko is funny first. The funnier he is, the worse the seeds land later.
- Nobody explains the boxes. Nobody explains Socko. Nobody says "Psygmund".
- Silence is a tool: after the attack, after the first shot, after the bolt slides home.
- **The colour goes near Marta.** The longer the player stays in a room with her, at the table or
  hunting, the more the world drains to black and white (about 75 s to none, easing in so the
  start goes unnoticed); it seeps back over about 25 s after leaving. Never explained, never
  mentioned by anyone, Socko included.
