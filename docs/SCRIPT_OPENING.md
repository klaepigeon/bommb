# HYSTERIA: the opening script (owner's text, canonical)

The owner's script, verbatim. Stage directions are in parentheses. The game must use these lines
as written. Where a line has no trigger yet in the new structure, the note after it says how the
rebuild plans to place it (a **PLAN**, open to change).

## Scene A

No intro text.

*Knocks.*

**Kurt:** Ms. Sokolov? Your sister asked me to come check on you.

*Knocks again.*

**Kurt:** Marta? It's Kurt. I'm coming in.

*Upon seeing the apartment:*

**Kurt:** Christ...

*Upon seeing Marta's body:*

**Kurt:** Oh... oh no.

*Upon investigating Marta's body:*

**Kurt:** What the hell is this?

**Marta:** Little boxes made of boxes with boxes inside of them... Have you seen them? The boxes, I mean. They're scary at first. But then you look at them for a moment

**Kurt:** Marta?

**Marta:** Look at the boxes, my little bird. Do you see them?

*Kurt gasps, knocked out.*

*Upon waking up:*

**Kurt:** Oh, jeez, that smarts *(rubbing his head)*

**Socko:** Heyyyy buddyyyy.... You should probably take it slow, a concussion is no joke!

**Kurt:** What the fuck!?

**Socko:** Who, me?

**Kurt:** Yeah, you. Who the fuck are you?

**Socko:** I'm Socko! I'm a handy little pal! Who are you?

**Kurt:** Uh... Kurt sounds right.

**Socko:** You don't know?

**Kurt:** I... yeah I got nothing.

**Socko:** Shouldn't have stood up so fast.

*Upon trying the (now shut) door they came through:*

**Kurt:** It won't open.

**Socko:** Maybe it's locked?

**Kurt:** Wow, thanks. I never would have guessed.

**Socko** *(genuine)***:** You're welcome! *(To himself)* I like helping.

*Kurt pushes the wardrobe.*

**Kurt:** What the hell Is that?

**Socko:** I don't know.

**Kurt:** Do you know anything?

**Socko:** I know that I can do this!

*(Socko goes cross-eyed.)*

**Socko:** Ooh! Ooh! And I can smell important stuff! Go this way!

*(Socko points toward the gun.)*

> **PLAN:** the hole behind the wardrobe is the way into the Backrooms (brief §6–7). Socko's
> "Go this way!" points toward where the gun lies in the Backrooms. Following him is the loud,
> violent route, which fits his agenda (brief §14).

## On the Flat 47 balcony

**Kurt:** You seeing this?

**Socko:** Is something unusual?

**Kurt:** To my recollection, typically there's more to the world than a sticky note that says "outside"

**Socko:** I wouldn't know, I'm more of an indoor person

> **PLAN:** this plays after waking (Socko is present), the first time Kurt steps onto the
> balcony. Beyond the railing there is nothing but a sticky note that says OUTSIDE. This is the
> first sign that the environment is unreliable, and it foreshadows Flat 48's rule (what something
> is, is what's written on it).

## Random conversations

These play at quiet moments: no threat nearby, no other line playing, and some time since the last one. Each plays once.

### A

**Kurt:** Hey, uh, Socko. I have a question.

**Socko:** What's up?

**Kurt:** Where did you come from?

**Socko:** Wow, what a great question, Kurt! When a mommy sock puppet and a daddy sock puppet love each other very much, they...

**Kurt:** Forget I asked.

### B

**Socko:** Hey, Kurt, can I ask you a question?

**Kurt:** Sure, Socko.

**Socko:** Thanks, I really appreciate that.

**Kurt:** What?

**Socko:** You're a tough crowd, Kurt.

### C

**Socko:** Kurt, have you ever had a sandwich?

**Kurt:** Uh, I think so

*(If Kurt has invested in memories:)* **Kurt:** Yeah.

**Socko:** What are they like?

*Memoryless Kurt:* **Kurt:** It's... Impossible to describe

*Kurt with 1 memory point invested:* **Kurt:** There's lots of different kinds of sandwiches, buddy. I'm gonna need you to be more specific.

**Socko** *(regardless)***:** Oh...

**Socko:** Could I be a sandwich?

**Kurt:** Probably not, buddy.

> **PLAN:** "memory points invested" requires a memory progression system. The rebuild records it
> as a state the dialogue system can query (`memory_points`). How Kurt earns memory points
> (discoveries in Flat 47 and the Backrooms) is to be designed with the consequence-tracking
> system (brief §11). Until it exists, the conversation uses the memoryless branch.

## Upon encountering Marta in the maze

**Marta:** *(clicks)*

**Socko:** What?

**Kurt:** Socko, buddy, what's happening?

**Socko:** How does she know you, Kurt?

> **PLAN:** "the maze" is the Backrooms. This plays at Kurt's first sighting of Marta as a hunter.
> Her clicking is also her audio signature for the rest of the hunt. Socko's "What?" is a
> reaction to the clicks: he understands her and Kurt can't (brief §5). That reaction is the
> mystery, and nothing explains it.
