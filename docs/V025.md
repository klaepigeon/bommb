# MEAT PARTY v0.2.6 / MURDERLAND (APK 3.5.0)

What changed since v0.2, and where it lives.

## Party foundation (v0.2.5 spec)
- **Board**: graph board (`board_graph.gd`) with branches, gates, district names; `board_rules.gd`
  handles path choices, shops, items, events, rule prompts, swaps, slides.
- **Economy**: Teeth + Dead Rats (`economy.gd`, `shop_manager.gd`, `shop.json`), items
  (`item_manager.gd`, `items.json`, three held), events (`events.json`), hidden rules for
  THE CALLER'S GAME mode (`rule_manager.gd`, `rules.json`), Caller's Awards and the finale
  (`award_manager.gd`, `victory_manager.gd`).
- **Match length / mode**: QUICK 10, STANDARD 15, EXTENDED 20, MARATHON 30 rounds; CLASSIC or
  THE CALLER'S GAME. Chosen in the lobby.
- **Saves**: the match checkpoints every round (`Saves` autoload); CONTINUE on the main menu
  resumes it offline with the same dice.
- **Facility**: free first-person walking away from the table (`facility.gd`, `facility.json`),
  with a return timer and Caller responses. **No chat anywhere** (tested).
- **Seven new minigames**, each with its own control (hold, swipe, timing ring, trace):
  FLOOR IS MEAT, HOOK LINE & SINKER, SPIN CYCLE, TUG OF WAR, THE CONTROLLER,
  LAST MAN BREATHING, DON'T PRESS IT.
- **UI**: party HUD (`hud.gd`, `party_ui.gd`), Titan One / Baloo 2 fonts (OFL), UI scale, button
  size, reduced effects, reduced gore, left-handed layout (`Ui` autoload).

## Addendum
- **Animation framework**: `BodyAnimator` clips (anticipation/action/impact/follow-through/
  recovery), meat puppets on the board and in ordinary minigames, full characters for special
  events; presentation modes MEAT_PUPPET / FULL_CHARACTER / CINEMATIC / CUSTOM.
- **Cutscenes** (`cutscene_manager.gd`): forced spectator scenes, once, target-only effects.
- **Results**: staged placement presentation with puppet reactions; Caller table animations.
- **ORGAN TRAIL**: 2v2 surgery special event (surgeon/patient, organs, complications, sabotage,
  modifiers). Never in the normal rotation: a surgery square or the Caller calls it.

## MURDERLAND
- **The venue**: the slaughterhouse as the robot's party (`party_venue.gd`): host stage,
  prize counter, buffet, arcade, escape-room door.
- **The board** (`murderland.json`, generated by `tools/make_murderland.py`): 7 districts,
  122 spaces, no crossing paths, the CONTROL ROOM spoke. Its own world, off the table, in a
  gritty industrial style: flat district grounds, steel walkways with hazard rails, outlined
  squares coloured by type, one accent colour per district (`murderland_board.gd`).
- **Control System** (`board_machines.gd`): signposts, floodgates, rotator, conveyors, the wheel,
  the death machine, the parade, the royal gate; changes are announced a round ahead.
- **Camera director** (`camera_director.gd`): prioritised shots with spring smoothing for
  the intro, turns, landings and minigames.
- **Arenas**: minigames play in 10 themed arenas away from the board (`arena_world.gd`).
- **Escape room**: 5 rooms off the hall (`escape_room.gd`, `data/hall/escape_rooms.json`);
  escaping is an ending.

Balance is untouched by design: all the numbers are first passes in JSON.

## 3.2.1
- **Touch fix**: the HUD's minigame-controls strip and action column were catching touches
  meant for the buttons under them (ROLL did nothing on phones). They now ignore input; the
  buttons inside still take it. `tests/click_probe.tscn` taps PLAY, SIT DOWN and ROLL with
  real screen touches at phone resolution and fails if any doesn't fire.
- **Look**: a Saw-style grade over everything (`saw` in `shaders/post.gdshader`,
  `Stage.saw_look()`): drained bile-green, crushed blacks, blood stays red. The UI is a
  typewriter face (Special Elite) on bone-coloured, hard-edged plates, with rust and blood
  accents instead of the candy colours.
- **Title**: the game is just MEAT PARTY (menu, app name, hall sign). The HAPPY BIRTHDAY banner is gone.

## 3.3.0
- **Dice 1-9**: a dice block over the roller's head flicks through 1-9 and lands. Legs still
  cost you: a broken leg -2 (painkillers cancel it), a prosthetic -1, a lost leg -3, never
  below 1-3. Loaded dice: LOW 1-4 / HIGH 6-9; loaded double 5-9 twice; the cheater's die is a 9.
- **Responsiveness**: buttons act on touch-down (except in scrolling lists) with a squash and a
  click; puppets hop square to square in time with the rules instead of trailing behind; shorter
  pauses (step 0.24 s, after a turn 0.9 s, intros 6 s, results 4 s); the AI thinks for 0.35-0.8 s;
  his lines type out twice as fast.
- **Party feedback**: a slammed-in banner for every turn, round, LAST ROUND, death, revival,
  caught cheat, lead change and announced Control System change (queued, never overlapping);
  the steps left count down over the mover's head; +/- Teeth and Dead Rats float up off the
  puppet.
- **HUD**: LAST CALL's hand-inked card stock, re-made as tintable paper (`assets/ink/party`):
  freehand ink border, coffee rings and specks, dyed from one palette. IM Fell English SC for
  headings and buttons, Special Elite for typed text (LAST CALL's fonts). The palette is planned:
  bone and dried-blood neutrals; blood red dominant; bile green and verdigris as its split
  complements; nicotine yellow for highlights; seats spaced round the wheel at matched value.
  The 3D grade is eased back so the board keeps its colour.
- **Input audit** (`tests/touch_bot.tscn`): plays a whole QUICK match by screen touches only,
  checking every visible input (buttons, hold/swipe/trace pads, timing rings, sticks) is the
  top control under its own centre and that every tapped button fires. It found and we fixed:
  the menu button hidden under the intro card; shop BUY/DONE scrolled off the bottom (the modal
  now fits the screen and DONE sits in a fixed footer); minigame cheat buttons overlapping the
  minigame controls (the action column now stacks above them).

## 3.4.0
- **Pacing like a party board game**: one steady high three-quarter camera on the board (same
  angle and field of view for every board shot; it glides after whoever's moving and never
  swings with the path or punches in); about 0.4 s a square; 1.8 s between turns; the AI takes
  1-1.7 s to decide; banners fade in place instead of whipping across.
- **The dice block** hangs over the roller's head flicking through 1-9 until ROLL hits it.
- **Puppets move on the squares**: they hop over the middle of every square they pass and only
  step aside to their spot once they stop.
- **The Caller doesn't talk.** He gestures. Every minigame opens on a HOW TO PLAY card: the
  rules, the controls, PRACTICE (the game's real controls, wired to "✓" feedback, nothing
  counts) and READY. The game starts when every person at the table is ready.
- **THE SEARCH** (7 SEARCH squares, one per district) replaces getting up whenever you like.
  Everyone at the table searches the showroom at once, in first person, for six scraps of
  paper; most found wins 10 Teeth. Then each finder secretly KEEPS (2 Teeth a scrap) or LAYS
  THEM ON THE TABLE. If all six were found and every finder laid theirs, the note is whole,
  "NOBODY WINS ALONE. PUT US BACK TOGETHER.", and everyone gets a Dead Rat. One keeper and it
  stays torn. The AI is more trusting once it has seen the note made whole. The escape room is
  reached during searches. (`scripts/core/search_event.gd`, `test_search`.)

## 3.5.0 (v0.2.6: game flow, pacing, the minigame framework)
See [FLOW.md](FLOW.md). A central game-flow state machine (ROUND_START ... GAME_END) with
legal transitions only; turns now have an announcement (a panel with the player's Teeth, Dead
Rats, pockets and afflictions), a landing beat (the square's name and what it does) and an
end; the round ends with its own announcement. Every minigame goes through one 15-stage
presentation controller (freeze, category, title + description, arena preview, teams, HOW TO
PLAY, CONTROLS with the real bindings and PRACTICE, arena, positioning, 3-2-1-GO, play,
FINISH, results, rewards, return). Results are shown before rewards are paid. Pacing presets
(standard / relaxed / fast) in the lobby. Real keyboard and controller bindings. A minigame
registration and validator; incomplete games are refused. The choice games no longer show a
stick they don't use. The Caller no longer speaks at all (no voice either).
